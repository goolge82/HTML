1. Afghanistan Defeats Zimbabwe in T20 International

Sharjah: Afghanistan defeated Zimbabwe by 19 runs in the second T20 International. Azmatullah Omarzai delivered an outstanding performance, scoring 116 runs and helping Afghanistan post 221/7. Rashid Khan also achieved a major milestone by becoming the first bowler to take 200 wickets in men's T20 internationals.

2. Prasidh Krishna Ruled Out of New Zealand ODI Series

New Delhi: Indian fast bowler Prasidh Krishna has been ruled out of the ODI series against New Zealand due to a hamstring injury. The development is a setback for India's bowling department as the team prepares for the upcoming matches.

3. England Defeats Sri Lanka in ODI Tri-Series

Lahore: England defeated Sri Lanka by 55 runs in the ODI tri-series. England scored 386 runs, with Ben Duckett producing an excellent innings of 167 runs. Sri Lanka fought back through Pavan Rathnayake, who scored 118, but eventually finished at 331.

4. Australia Gains Advantage in Test Against South Africa

Australian batter Josh Inglis scored a century in the third Test against South Africa, helping Australia gain an advantage. Australia posted 429 runs in its first innings. South Africa continued its second innings at 140/4, trailing Australia by 40 runs.

5. India Secures Victory in Asian Champions Trophy

Sports Desk: India produced an impressive performance against China, securing a 5–2 victory in the Asian Champions Trophy. Harmanpreet Singh and Sukhjeet Singh scored two goals each, helping India dominate the match.

6. Daniil Medvedev Defeats Cameron Norrie in Vienna

Vienna: Russian tennis player Daniil Medvedev defeated Cameron Norrie after saving a match point and overcoming a difficult situation. The victory marked his 100th tour-level quarter-final appearance.

7. India Prepares for Fourth T20I Against New Zealand

India will face New Zealand in the fourth T20 International. India has already secured the five-match series with a 3–0 lead. The Indian team will look to continue its winning performance, while New Zealand will aim to register its first victory in the series.
