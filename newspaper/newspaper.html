1. CYCLONE ARNAB BATTERS ANDHRA COAST

Heavy Rainfall, Strong Winds Cause Widespread Damage; Thousands Evacuated

Visakhapatnam, October 29: Cyclone Arnab has caused significant damage across coastal Andhra Pradesh after making landfall near Narsapur in West Godavari district early Wednesday. The severe weather brought heavy rainfall and powerful winds, damaging houses, roads, electricity infrastructure and agricultural fields. According to reports, at least three people lost their lives in cyclone-related incidents.Several areas experienced flooding and power disruptions, while uprooted trees blocked roads and affected transportation. Agricultural land and plantations also suffered considerable damage.

Thousands Shifted to Safer Locations

Authorities evacuated more than 84,000 people to cyclone shelters in Andhra Pradesh as a precautionary measure. Disaster response teams, police personnel and other emergency workers were deployed to assist affected residents. Arrangements were made to provide food, drinking water, medical care and other essential supplies to people in shelters. The state administration also directed officials to clear fallen trees, restore electricity and protect irrigation tanks and canals from overflowing.

Heavy Rainfall Warning Continues

Although Cyclone Arnab weakened after moving inland, its impact is expected to continue through heavy rainfall in several regions. The India Meteorological Department has warned of further rainfall and possible flooding in vulnerable areas. Neighbouring states may also experience adverse weather conditions.Residents in low-lying areas have been advised to remain alert, avoid flooded roads and follow official weather advisories. Fishermen have been urged to follow safety instructions and avoid entering the sea during dangerous conditions.Government Focuses on Relief and Recovery

The authorities are assessing damage to homes, infrastructure and crops while continuing relief and restoration operations. Emergency services remain prepared to respond to further incidents caused by heavy rain and flooding.The cyclone highlights the importance of timely weather warnings, disaster preparedness and coordinated rescue operations in protecting lives and reducing property damage.
